BIO

Jon Ayerdi is a researcher and lecturer at the Software and Systems Engineering Group (SSE) at the University of Mondragon, Spain. He defended his PhD thesis at the University of Mondragon in 2022, and has written over 10 peer-reviewed conference and journal articles. He has also reviewed for various scientific journals. His research interests are in software verification, particularly automatic verification of cyber-physical systems and machine learning based systems, as well as techniques based on metamorphic testing.
